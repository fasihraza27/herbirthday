one last thing

For the days you forget how loved you are.

My girl,

I wanted to write this one slowly, and without any jokes hiding in it.

There will be days when you forget how loved you are. Days when you're tired, or everything feels heavy, or you feel like you're not enough. On those days, I want these words to be waiting for you right here.

You deserve love. Not the kind you have to earn by being perfect, or strong, or cheerful. Just love, exactly as you are.

You deserve kindness, from the people around you and from yourself too. Please be as gentle with yourself as you would be with someone you care about.

You deserve happiness, the real kind, not the kind you put on for other people. And you deserve peace, quiet days where your heart gets to rest.

You don't have to pretend to be okay all the time. You don't have to smile when you don't feel like it, or explain every feeling, or have everything figured out. If today is a heavy day, then it's allowed to be a heavy day.

Your feelings matter. All of them, the happy ones and the hard ones. They are not too much, and they are not a burden. You never have to carry them quietly.

So if you ever forget everything else, remember this: you are loved, you are enough, and you matter.

Happy birthday, my girl. — Fasih
